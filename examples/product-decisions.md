# Product Decision Examples

## Buy versus build

**Request:** “Both vendors and an internal build are feasible. Which should we choose?”

**Path:** `steelman-decision`

Steelman each option under the same horizon and criteria. Likely flip variables include differentiation value, switching cost, integration burden, internal capability, vendor survivability, and time to market. Ask one question only if its answer can reverse the recommendation.

## Untested demand

**Request:** “Should we spend three months building the enterprise integration?”

**Path:** `minimum-experiment`

If the critical uncertainty is willingness to adopt or pay, design a bounded test that produces behavioral evidence before implementation. A credible test might be a paid design-partner commitment with a manual service behind a realistic interface. Precommit to thresholds and do not count compliments as demand.

## Cross-functional launch

**Request:** “Engineering says it is ready, legal is concerned, and sales needs it this quarter.”

**Path:** `expert-panel -> steelman-decision`

The panel separates feasibility, regulatory exposure, customer commitments, and revenue timing. It surfaces which disagreements are factual and which reflect different loss functions. The decision skill then weights launch, limited rollout, or delay without pretending those weights are facts.
