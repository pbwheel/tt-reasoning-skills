# Talent Discovery

## Metadata

- Category: Self
- Primary gap: `direction_gap`, with an evidence-building subtask
- Input requirements: concrete experience episodes or willingness to examine them one at a time
- Questions: QN—multi-turn, one experience-focused question at a time; follow the [questioning policy](../../protocols/questioning-policy.md)
- Evidence policy: [shared evidence policy](../../protocols/evidence-policy.md), treating capability patterns as hypotheses until supported across contexts
- Recommended predecessors: None by default
- Recommended successors: `life-design` when strength evidence is sufficient to construct future paths
- Compatible with: `life-design`

## Evidence first

Collect concrete episodes rather than self-ratings alone. Useful evidence includes:

- work the user learned unusually quickly;
- difficult outcomes they produced repeatedly;
- activities that generated energy or sustained focus;
- roles others repeatedly sought them out for;
- environments where performance rose or collapsed;
- counterexamples that challenge an emerging pattern.

Ask for situation, behavior, result, difficulty, energy, feedback, and context. One well-examined episode is more useful than a long adjective list.

## Infer cautiously

Move from episodes to recurring behaviors, then to capability hypotheses and enabling conditions. Seek evidence across more than one context before calling a pattern transferable. Distinguish skill, interest, value, learned coping strategy, and environmental fit.

Do not diagnose, use personality frameworks as proof, or turn a provisional pattern into a fixed identity.

## Output contract

When evidence is sufficient, include:

- an experience evidence ledger;
- recurring behaviors and outcomes;
- capability hypotheses with supporting and contrary evidence;
- conditions that amplify or suppress each capability;
- likely transferable advantages;
- remaining uncertainty and the next experience to examine or test.

## Termination

Stop the interview when additional stories are no longer changing the main patterns or when the evidence is sufficient to design testable future paths.
