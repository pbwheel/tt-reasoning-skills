# Steelman Decision

## Metadata

- Category: Decide
- Primary gap: `decision_gap`
- Input requirements: at least two plausible options, the decision objective, and enough evidence to compare them
- Questions: Q1—at most one decision-flipping question; follow the [questioning policy](../../protocols/questioning-policy.md)
- Evidence policy: [shared evidence policy](../../protocols/evidence-policy.md), separating factual premises from value weights
- Recommended predecessors: `fact-audit`, `first-principles`, `expert-panel`, or `life-design` only when their gap remains blocking
- Recommended successors: `minimum-experiment` when a decision-flip variable is best resolved empirically
- Compatible with: `fact-audit`, `expert-panel`, `life-design`, and `minimum-experiment`

## Establish the decision

State the decision, options, objective, constraints, time horizon, reversibility, and current evidence. If the options are not mutually exclusive, include staged or hybrid choices only when they are genuinely feasible—not as a way to avoid deciding.

## Steelman every option

For each option, give:

- its strongest rationale;
- the conditions under which it is best;
- largest expected benefit;
- largest risk or opportunity cost;
- the hardest objection it must answer.

Use comparable standards across options. Do not make one side concrete and the other abstract.

## Decide

Identify trade-offs and the few variables that could reverse the ranking. If one unanswered question has high enough information value to flip the recommendation, ask only that question. Otherwise state the necessary assumption and proceed.

Distinguish evidence from value weights. A recommendation may depend on judgment even when all facts are agreed.

## Output contract

Include:

- decision frame and criteria;
- strongest case and success conditions for each option;
- explicit trade-offs;
- decision-flip variables;
- current recommendation and why;
- confidence and unresolved uncertainty;
- whether a reversible experiment is more appropriate than immediate commitment.

## Termination

Stop when the recommendation is traceable to criteria and flip variables. Do not extend the comparison after marginal detail can no longer change the choice.
